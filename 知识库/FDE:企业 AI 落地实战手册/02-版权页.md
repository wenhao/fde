## 术语说明 | Terminology Note

本书中 FDE（Forward Deployed Engineer）的中文全称译为**"前线部署工程师"**；招聘语境中也常称"前沿部署工程师"。正文首次出现处会同时标注两种说法，其后统一简称 FDE。

In this book, FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer) is translated as "前线部署工程师"; in recruiting contexts it is also often called "前沿部署工程师". Both terms are noted on first mention in each chapter; subsequently abbreviated as FDE.
